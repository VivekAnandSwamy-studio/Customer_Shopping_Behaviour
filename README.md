# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing customer shopping behavior, identifying purchasing patterns, and generating business insights using **Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes transactional data from **3,900 customer purchases** across various product categories.

The analysis focuses on understanding:

* Customer spending patterns
* Product preferences
* Customer segments
* Subscription behavior
* Discount usage
* Shipping preferences
* Revenue contribution across customer groups

The objective is to transform raw customer transaction data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making.

## 🐍 1. Python — Data Preparation & EDA

Python was used to prepare and explore the dataset before performing the SQL analysis.

### Key Steps

* Loaded the dataset using Pandas
* Explored the dataset structure using `df.info()`
* Reviewed statistical summaries using `df.describe()`
* Checked for missing values
* Imputed missing `Review Rating` values using the median rating of each product category
* Standardized column names using `snake_case`
* Created an `age_group` column based on customer age
* Created a `purchase_frequency_days` column
* Checked whether `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` contained redundant information
* Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column
* Connected Python to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned dataset

---

## 🗄️ 2. PostgreSQL & SQL Analysis

The cleaned dataset was loaded into PostgreSQL for structured business analysis.

The following business questions were analyzed:

### Revenue & Customer Analysis

* Compared revenue generated by male and female customers
* Identified customers who used discounts while spending above the average purchase amount
* Compared subscribers and non-subscribers based on average spending and total revenue
* Analyzed revenue contribution by age group

### Product Analysis

* Identified the top 5 products based on average review rating
* Found products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases
* Identified the top 3 most purchased products within each category

### Customer Behavior

* Segmented customers into **New, Returning, and Loyal** segments based on purchase history
* Analyzed whether customers with more than 5 purchases were more likely to subscribe
* Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping

## 💡 Business Recommendations

The analysis resulted in several business recommendations:

### 1. Increase Subscription Adoption

Promote exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage customers to subscribe.

### 2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty

Introduce loyalty initiatives that reward repeat buyers and encourage movement toward the Loyal customer segment.

### 3. Review Discount Strategy

Evaluate the use of discounts while maintaining control over business margins.

### 4. Improve Product Positioning

Use top-rated and best-selling products in promotional campaigns.

### 5. Target High-Value Customer Groups

Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and customers using express shipping.
